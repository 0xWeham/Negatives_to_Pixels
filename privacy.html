Privacy Policy

Negatives to Pixels · Effective 7 July 2026

Negatives to Pixels converts photographs of film negatives into positive images. It is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

The short version

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything the app does happens locally on your iPad.

What data the app handles

Your photos. When you import an image, you choose it through Apple's system photo picker. The app only ever receives the specific images you select — it has no access to the rest of your photo library. Imported images and your edits are stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else.

Saving your conversions. When you export a converted image to your photo library, the app asks for add-only permission — it can save new images but cannot read, modify, or delete anything else in your library. You can also export to the Files app instead, which requires no photo library permission at all.

What we collect

Nothing.

  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code of any kind.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no personal information to provide.
  • No network activity. The app does not send your images or any other data over the internet. Image processing (negative conversion, editing, export) is performed entirely on-device.
  • No third-party services. The app includes no third-party SDKs.

External links

The app's Settings screen contains a link to our Instagram profile. Tapping it opens Instagram (the app or your browser), which is operated by Meta and governed by Meta's privacy policy. We receive no information about whether or how you use that link.

Data retention and deletion

Your imported images and edits remain on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app itself. Deleting the app permanently removes all of its data. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our end.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.
